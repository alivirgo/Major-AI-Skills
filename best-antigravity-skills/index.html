Best Antigravity Skills

Antigravity watches ~/.agents/skills. The best Antigravity skills setup is a small, reviewed set—not a full-library dump.

Safety rule: Major AI Skills requires --skills, --category, or explicit --all for Antigravity so you do not exhaust context.
npx major-ai-skills --antigravity --skills blender,ffmpeg,docker --dry-run
npx major-ai-skills --agy --skills blender,ffmpeg

  • figma, vscode — design/dev
