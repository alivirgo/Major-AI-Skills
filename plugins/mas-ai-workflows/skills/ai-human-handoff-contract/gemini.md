# AI Human Handoff Contract (Gemini)

Audit whether humans receive enough context without a full data dump. Flag single global confidence thresholds, missing never-answer lists, and any flow that treats timeout as yes. Prefer clear stop/route/escalate messaging to users.

> **MANDATORY**: Reject silent consent patterns; require bound approvals for irreversible acts.
