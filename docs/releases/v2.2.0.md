# Major AI Skills 2.2.0

402 canonical skills, eight focused Claude/Codex bundles, and synchronized Gemini extension metadata.

## Ten new AI workflows

- `ai-evaluation-dataset`: held-out datasets and leakage checks.
- `rag-retrieval-audit`: evidence retrieval and failure attribution.
- `llm-json-contract-check`: syntax, schema, and business-rule validation.
- `ai-citation-verification`: claim-to-source support review.
- `ai-pii-redaction-review`: minimized inputs and privacy leakage checks.
- `agent-injection-boundary-test`: authorized sandbox tests with harmless canaries.
- `agent-tool-replay-test`: deterministic tool replay and side-effect boundaries.
- `prompt-regression-gate`: paired prompt comparisons and release thresholds.
- `llm-cost-latency-benchmark`: observed usage, latency, and quality tradeoffs.
- `ai-human-handoff-contract`: explicit approval states and review packets.
