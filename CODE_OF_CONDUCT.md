# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in Major AI Skills a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information
- Trolling or sustained disruption
- Anything illegal or clearly malicious

## Enforcement

Report concerns via GitHub Issues or by contacting the maintainers through the repository. Maintainers may remove comments, close PRs, or ban contributors who violate this code.
