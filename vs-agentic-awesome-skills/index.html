Major AI Skills vs Agentic Awesome Skills

Both are installable agentic skills / SKILL.md libraries for Claude Code, Cursor, and related agents. They optimize for different jobs.

Bottom line: Use Major AI Skills for professional app automation and token-efficiency skills. Use agentic-awesome-skills when you want the broadest coding/security/DevOps catalog and AAS Core tooling.
